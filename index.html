About

About Me

I came to Salesforce through systems work and stayed because it rewards people who understand both the business process and the data underneath it. My experience covers Salesforce development and administration, business and systems analysis, and analytics work in SQL, Python, and Power BI.

I am now adding a careful study of AI in enterprise systems, including Agentforce and Data 360, and testing what those tools can honestly do inside a real workflow.

I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science from Texas A&M University Corpus Christi, completed in 2024, and I am based in Baltimore, Maryland. Across roles at Perficient, Intellibee, and Accenture, and earlier Salesforce roles in India with IBM and SportsPlus, I have worked in Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Health Cloud, and Experience Cloud environments.

What I reach for first is evidence: the requirement, the data, the validation check. If a number or a feature cannot be traced back to those, it does not belong in a report or a release.

How I Approach Problems

From problem to documented result

1Understand the problem

In the healthcare case study, the work starts with a written business problem and the stakeholders it affects.

2Examine requirements and data

In the K 12 project, profiling the source tables came before any analysis, and it changed the population the whole project rests on.

3Design before building

CaseAssist began with a data model, so the Apex services and the agent layer have a stable structure to build on.

4Build and validate

Fifteen validation checks run on the K 12 processed tables, and every headline number is reconciled across tools before it is quoted.

5Document the outcome

Each project keeps its questions, decisions, and limitations in writing, so a reviewer can retrace the work without asking me.
